Dataset characteristics (Reviewer 2, Comment #25)

Definitions
- Options: feature columns of data/<system>.csv with more than one distinct value. Columns with a
  single value (2 each in DUNE, HIPACC, HSMGP, KANZI) are excluded from all counts and from p.
- Binary option: 2 distinct values. Numeric option: more than 2 distinct values.
  Several binary columns are one-hot encodings of one categorical option (e.g. Smoother_* in HSMGP,
  blocksize_* in KANZI, compression* in LRZIP, ref_* in X264, PS*K in BDBC).
- S_i/p: training size divided by the number of options p.
- Skewness: third standardized moment of the performance value. CV: standard deviation / mean.
- eta squared of an option: between-group sum of squares of the performance value over the groups
  defined by the values of that option, divided by the total sum of squares. Computed on all
  configurations, one option at a time; no model is fitted. Because options are correlated
  (one-hot groups, constraints), the values of different options do not add up to a decomposition
  and their sum can exceed 1 (X264: ref_1 and ref_9 are 0.64 each).

Observations
1. Sampling density at S1 splits the systems in two groups: APACHE, BDBC, KANZI and X264 have about
   1.1 to 1.2 training configurations per option (KANZI lowest, 1.07), while HSMGP (6.4), HIPACC (8.4),
   LRZIP (10.6) and DUNE (24.9) have between 6 and 25.
2. HSMGP has the most concentrated effect: one option (Smoother_GSACBE) alone explains 0.76 of the
   variance, no other option exceeds 0.05, and the top 3 reach 0.85.
3. LRZIP has the most dispersed performance values (CV 2.18, skewness 3.60). One binary option
   (compressionZpaq) explains 0.50; the next is a numeric option with 9 levels (level, 0.09).
4. KANZI has the second highest CV (1.98) and the variance is spread over more options than in any
   other system: 4 options exceed 0.05 and the largest explains 0.33. It has only binary options and
   the lowest S1/p.
5. HSMGP, LRZIP and KANZI are three of the four systems with CV above 1 (1.33, 2.18, 1.98); the fourth
   is BDBC (1.42). The remaining systems have CV between 0.34 and 0.44. Skewness does not separate
   them: DUNE has the highest skewness (4.73) with a CV of 0.34.
6. Numeric options exist only in DUNE, HIPACC, HSMGP and LRZIP, with at most 3 per system and a share
   of at most one third of the options.
